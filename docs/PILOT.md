# LostBox pilot kit

Everything needed to run a one- to two-week pilot with a school's front office
(SPEC Section 12). Print this, or share the link with the staff contact.

## What LostBox does

When something is turned in, staff take a quick photo and log it. Students scan
a poster, see what's been found, and send a short claim ("it has my initials
inside the lid"). Staff check the claim against the item, approve it, and hand
the item over in person. Items nobody claims show up on a "Ready to donate"
list.

It replaces "come dig through the box" with "check your phone first".

## What it collects

- **Found items:** a photo, the kind of item, its colors, a short note, and where
  and when it was found. Staff can also add private notes (only staff see them).
- **Claims:** the detail a student types to prove an item is theirs, and an
  email address only if they choose to give one.
- **Staff accounts:** the email each staff member signs in with.
- **School settings:** name, campus places, pickup location and hours, and
  privacy and retention settings.

## What it does not collect

- No student names, student ID numbers, or student accounts. Students never sign
  up or log in.
- No ID uploads, ever.
- No location data. GPS is stripped from every photo.
- No ads, tracking, or analytics. Nothing is sold or shared.

Photos of returned, donated, or removed items, and contact emails on closed
claims, are deleted automatically after the number of days the school chooses (7 by default). The full plain-English
policy is at `/privacy` in the app.

## Daily staff workflow

| When | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| An item is turned in | Photo, category, colors, where found. Tick **Private item** for valuables. | **Add** (the + button) |
| A few times a day | Open **Claims**. Compare what the student wrote with the item and its private notes. **Approve** or **Reject**. | **Claims** (the badge shows how many are waiting) |
| A student comes to pick up | Check their detail again in person, hand it over, press **Mark picked up and returned**. | **Claims → Ready for pickup** |
| Once a week | Review old items and mark the ones going to donation. | **Items → "N items are ready to donate"** |
| Anytime | See how many things got home. | **Impact** |

**Approximate time:** about 30 seconds to log an item, about 1 minute per
claim, and 2 to 3 minutes for the weekly donate review. A typical day with 5
new items and 3 claims is **under 10 minutes**. This is an estimate; the survey
below measures the real number.

## 10-minute staff walkthrough

Do this on the staff member's own phone, with the demo school (join code
`DEMO2026`) or your school once it's approved.

1. **(1 min) Why.** "Students check their phone before coming to the office. You
   stay in control: nothing goes back without you checking in person."
2. **(2 min) Log an item.** Tap **+**, take a photo, pick the category and color,
   choose where it was found, save. Show the **Private item** switch: the photo
   stays hidden from students.
3. **(2 min) The student side.** On a second phone, scan the poster, find the
   item, and press **This might be mine**. Type a detail and send. Point out the
   claim code the student gets.
4. **(2 min) Decide the claim.** Open **Claims**: the student's words sit next to
   the item and its private notes. Approve it. Explain that the real check
   happens at the desk.
5. **(1 min) Pickup.** **Ready for pickup → Mark picked up and returned.**
6. **(1 min) Donate list and Impact.** Show where old items collect, and the return
   numbers.
7. **(1 min) Questions.** Leave the poster and the `/privacy` link.

## 3-question staff survey

Ask at the end of the pilot (anonymous; paper or a form).

1. **About how many minutes a day did LostBox take you?**
   (0–5 / 5–10 / 10–20 / more than 20)
2. **Compared with how lost and found worked before, LostBox made returning items:**
   (much harder / a bit harder / about the same / a bit easier / much easier)
3. **What's one thing you'd change before using it all year?** (free text)

Count the answers and quote question 3 only with permission and without names.

## Before the pilot (checklist)

- [ ] Permission from the front office or an administrator, and a teacher sponsor.

- [ ] Baseline: how many items are in the box now, and roughly how many get

- [ ] The walkthrough done with everyone who will log items.
